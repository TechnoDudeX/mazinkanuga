01

Chikoo Banana with Parle-G

Green lid · Natrel Plus 2% · 24 hr freeze
47 g protein1 batch · 2 servings

Ripe chikoo, banana, and a milk-biscuit mix-in. The fruit-forward pint in this week’s rotation.

01 / Ingredients

  • After spinning · mix-in

Keep the scale for milk, fruit, whey, and pudding mix. For 1/16 tsp gum, use half of a level ⅛ tsp.

02 / The method

  1. Weigh the base

    Add the milk, peeled and seeded chikoo, banana, whey, dry banana mix, allulose, and vanilla to a blender. Sprinkle the xanthan onto the dry powders before blending.

  2. Blend until completely smooth

    Blend for 60 seconds. Scrape down the sides, then blend for 15 seconds more. There should be no fruit pieces or powder pockets left.

  3. Fill, label, and freeze for 24 hours

    Pour into your CREAMi container immediately, keeping below its MAX FILL line. Use the matching lid and label the container body too. Freeze upright on a level shelf for at least 24 hours.

  4. LITE ICE CREAM, then re-spin if needed

    Remove the storage lid and assemble your machine according to its guide. Run LITE ICE CREAM. If the result is powdery, add 10 g of milk and run RE-SPIN. Stop when smooth.

  5. MIX-IN the Parle-G

    Make a hole down the centre. Add 2 Parle-G biscuits (11 g), broken into large pieces. Run MIX-IN once and serve immediately.

Use fully ripe chikoo. It should give to gentle pressure. Peel it and remove every seed before weighing the flesh. Blend thoroughly for a smooth base.

Less allulose in this one. Start with 1½ tsp because the chikoo and banana already bring sweetness. Taste the liquid base before adjusting.

Strawberry · Natrel base

Pink lid · Natrel Plus 2% · 24 hr freeze
46 g protein1 batch · 2 servings

Fresh strawberries, strawberry dessert mix, and a little lemon. This is the new Natrel version; the original Fairlife recipe is here too.

Keep the scale for milk, fruit, whey, and pudding mix. For 1/16 tsp gum, use half of a level ⅛ tsp.

Fresh or frozen berries. Use ripe, flavourful berries. If using frozen strawberries, thaw and drain them before weighing.

Keep the lemon. The 1 tsp of lemon juice brightens the strawberry flavour. Visible strawberry seeds are normal.

  3. Taste the tang

05

Banana Pudding

Original recipe · Fairlife 2% · 24 hr freeze
40 g proteinOriginal batch · listed as 1 half pint

The original banana-and-vanilla half-pint recipe. Banana and pudding mix do the thickening; no xanthan gum.

01 / Ingredients

  • Optional mix-in

Original ingredient amounts and method from the recipe collection.

02 / The method

  1. Weigh everything into the jar

    Blender jar on the scale, zero it, and add the lot. One jar, one dish to wash.

  2. Blend · 60 sec, then 15 more

    Stop, push everything down off the sides, and give it another 15 seconds. Check it — fully smooth, no flecks. The machine will not break down banana pieces later, so whatever texture you leave here is the texture you get.

  3. Taste before it goes in

    Add more allulose only if it isn't sweet. Frozen always reads less sweet than the liquid does, but overdoing it here is how you end up with soup.

  4. Into the pint immediately

    Cut banana browns in air. Don't leave it sitting in the blender while you tidy up.

  5. Freeze 24 hours, level

    Lid on, flat shelf. A pint that freezes at an angle comes out dry on one side.

  6. Spin on LITE ICE CREAM

    Freezer to machine, no detours — don't let the pint start softening on the counter.

  7. Re-spin

    First pass comes out dry and powdery. That's normal. Add 10 ml of milk and RE-SPIN, again if it needs it. Two cycles is standard once there's whey in the base.

  8. Mix-ins

    Hole down the center, wafers in, MIX-IN once. One cycle only.

  9. Serve immediately

    It doesn't hold. Eat it out of the pint like everyone else does.

Troubleshooting. Dry and powdery — that's just the first spin. 10 ml milk, RE-SPIN. Elastic or stringy — you added gum on top of the starch. Don't. Soupy — warm pint or too much allulose. Give it the full 24 hours. Banana pieces — under-blended. 60 seconds, minimum.

Strawberry · original

Original recipe · Fairlife 2% · 24 hr freeze
40 g proteinOriginal batch · listed as 1 half pint

The original Fairlife strawberry recipe, with ½ tsp vanilla and ⅛ tsp xanthan gum.

01 / Ingredients

Original ingredient amounts and method from the recipe collection.

02 / The method

  1. Blend · 60 sec

    Everything into the blender at once, with the xanthan gum sprinkled onto the dry powders, not into the milk — that's what keeps it from lumping. Run it for a full minute.

  2. Into the pint immediately

    The starch in the mix starts to set in 3 to 5 minutes. Pour it before it does.

  3. Freeze 24 hours, level

    Lid on, flat shelf. A pint that freezes at an angle comes out dry on one side.

  4. Spin on LITE ICE CREAM

    Straight from the freezer into the machine.

  5. Re-spin if needed

    If it comes out dry, add 10 ml of milk and RE-SPIN.

No extra salt. The mix has 284 mg sodium per 12 g · that's enough

Why 12 g, not the box ratio. The box calls for 48 g in 500 ml of milk. That makes a pudding that holds its shape — in a frozen base it turns the ice cream thick and pasty. One serving is right for a half pint.

Chocolate Banana

Original recipe · Fairlife 2% · 24 hr freeze
41 g proteinOriginal batch · listed as 1 half pint

Chocolate up front, ripe banana in the background. The original Fairlife recipe with a small pinch of xanthan gum.

01 / Ingredients

Original ingredient amounts and method from the recipe collection.

02 / The method

  1. Weigh everything into the jar

    Blender jar on the scale, zero it. Milk in, cut the banana straight into the jar, then the whey and the pudding mix.

  2. Xanthan on top of the powders

    Allulose and vanilla in. Sprinkle the xanthan gum onto the dry powders, not into the milk — that's what keeps it from lumping.

  3. Blend · 75 sec, then 15 more

    Stop, push everything down off the sides, and give it another 15 seconds. Fully smooth, no flecks — the machine will not break down banana pieces later.

  4. Taste before it goes in

    Add more allulose only if it isn't sweet. Frozen always reads less sweet than the liquid does.

  5. Into the pint immediately

    The starch in the mix starts to set in 3 to 5 minutes, and cut banana browns in air. Don't let it sit.

  6. Freeze 24 hours, level

    Lid on, flat shelf. A pint that freezes at an angle comes out dry on one side.

  7. Spin on LITE ICE CREAM

    Freezer to machine, no detours — don't let the pint start softening on the counter.

  8. Re-spin

    Dry or in pieces? Add 10 ml of milk and RE-SPIN, again if it needs it. Two cycles is standard with whey. Stop when the surface is smooth.

  9. Serve immediately

    For mix-ins, hole down the center, mix-ins in, MIX-IN once. One cycle only.

No extra salt. The mix brings 330 mg sodium per 10 g · that's enough

Xanthan gum. It's a sixteenth of a teaspoon on purpose. The pudding mix already has xanthan in it and the banana adds starch. If the pint comes out elastic or stringy, use none in the next one.

More banana. Chocolate is dominant and banana sits in the background. Go up to 80 g of banana for more of it — don't increase the mix.

Without the whey. Drop the protein powder and use 290 ml of milk. About 255 calories and 17 g of protein.

One prep. Four pints.

The weekly routine

01 / PREP DAY

Blend each base.

Weigh one recipe at a time. Blend until smooth, then pour below your container’s MAX FILL line. Leave the biscuits for later.

02 / THE WAIT

Label. Freeze. Forget.

Match the lid colour and label the container body too. Freeze level for at least 24 hours. The lid comes off when you spin; the label stays.

03 / WHEN YOU WANT IT

Spin, then mix in.

LITE ICE CREAM first. Dry? Add 10 g milk and RE-SPIN. Green gets Parle-G; grey and teal get Biscoff; pink stays biscuit-free.
