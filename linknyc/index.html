07 · Delivery

The walkthrough script

Roughly two and a half minutes. Cut the middle if the call is running long — the asks at the end matter more.

Open — the split

“My slide is the email programme. Everything on it comes from one decision: a person is either registered or they are not, and those two groups never get the same email. Eleven sends go to people who have not registered. Twelve go to people who have.”

Where we are

“Right now that is 35 BCM RSVPs against a 450 target, and about 140 registrations across all sources. Yesterday’s send opened at 8 per cent against a 28 per cent benchmark. The cause was ours — we sent an invitation to a one-room event in New York without a geography filter. That is fixed from Thursday.”

The seven types — the substance of the slide

“There are seven kinds of email in here and each one does exactly one job.

  • 1 · ProgrammeProgramme emails give a reason to care, one panel per email. Three of them: The Road Ahead, which is the CEO panel; market hours to market infrastructure, which is the one capital markets will care about; and liquidity standards, which answers ‘how does this make me money’. That is the middle of the whole cadence, and it is content rather than reminders.
  • 2 · NetworkingNetworking emails say the room is the product. That came from a real APAC client who told Resham the introductions are what he actually wants. The subject line is ‘join peers from’ and then we name institutions.
  • 3 · RegionalRegional emails are new this week. Every Sibos wave is now three sends — David to Europe, Frank to the US, Nikki to APAC — in their own time zone, with a case study from their own market, from a named human rather than the brand. Whether we do the same on Link NYC is an open question and I will come back to it.
  • 4 · ScarcityScarcity emails force the decision, and they only work if the number is true.
  • 5 · Personal / VIPPersonal and VIP emails strip out every marketing signal. Three lines, plain text, from one person, asking for a reply instead of a click. On Sibos that is a note from Sergey and the replies go to Amal’s team.
  • 6 · LogisticsLogistics emails carry no argument at all. Doors, transit, check-in, dress code, a phone number that answers. They exist so nobody has a reason not to arrive.
  • 7 · Post-eventPost-event splits honestly — people who came, people who registered and did not, and everyone else. The strongest asset in the whole plan is the five-best-moments email with clips.”
Close — two asks and a date

“Everything that sends between 1 and 10 September is built and scheduled by 31 August, so nothing waits on me being online. Two things are blocked, and I need them from this call.”

08 · Blockers

The two asks —
say the name

  1. 01
    Amal — the real seat cap, in writing

    Two people have already drafted “only 50 places left” copy and nobody owns that number. Scarcity that is not true is the kind of thing a client notices.

  2. 02
    Hod — where the clips live

    Website, YouTube or LinkedIn. The five-best-moments email is the highest-value post-event asset in the plan and it cannot be built until that is one decision.

Bank quietly, off the call

Justin’s daily speaker drops and the speaker email should carry the same name on the same day. Catch him afterwards rather than spending slide time on it.

09 · Q&A

If you are challenged

They say You say
“Isn’t 23 emails spam?”“Nobody receives 23. The most any one person gets is 11, and the moment they register the invitations stop. That is what the split is for.”
“Why not one big email with everything in it?”“We tried it. The first Sibos send went to 500 matched accounts and produced one meeting. One email carrying five arguments is one email nobody finishes.”
“Why 8 per cent?”“No geography filter on a New York event, and a colder list. Both fixed from Thursday. I would rather report it than have somebody find it.”
“Are we going to hit 450?”“Not on the current list. That is a list problem, not a cadence problem, and I have four ways to close it that need a decision from Amal, Johan and Hod.”
“Can we put the Link NYC link in the Sibos emails?”“Ashley ruled that out yesterday — Link NYC is invite only and the Sibos audience is more junior. A VIP-only exception is still open.”
