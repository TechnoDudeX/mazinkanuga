The headline: CCIP 2.0 is live

Chainlink launched CCIP 2.0 at Sibos. The upgrade gives banks, asset managers and market infrastructures more control over how tokenized assets move across public and private blockchains:

  • Institutional risk controls: institutions can run their own Cross-Chain Verifiers, or select third-party providers, on top of CCIP's default security.
  • Configurable settlement: faster transfers for high-frequency payments, or full source-chain finality for higher-value settlement.
  • Built-in compliance: Chainlink's Automated Compliance Engine (ACE) lets issuers embed eligibility checks, transfer limits and other policies directly into cross-chain transfers.

Institutions set these frameworks once and apply them across additional blockchains, without separate infrastructure for each network.
