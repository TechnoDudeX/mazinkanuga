Mazin's Strawberry Ninja Creami

Protein ice cream · 1 half pint

Ingredients

  • Fairlife 2% milk 280 ml
  • Strawberries, hulled 100 g
  • Vanilla whey 33 g
  • Strawberry dessert mix 12 g
  • Allulose 1 tbsp
  • Vanilla extract ½ tsp
  • No salt · no xanthan gum

Steps

  1. Blend it all for 60 sec
  2. Into the pint right away, it sets in 3–5 min
  3. Freeze level for 24 hrs
  4. Spin on LITE ICE CREAM
  5. Dry? Add 10 ml milk, RE-SPIN
