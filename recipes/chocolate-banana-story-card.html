Mazin's Choco Banana Ninja Creami

Protein ice cream · 1 half pint

Ingredients

  • Fairlife 2% milk 305 ml
  • Ripe banana 60 g
  • Vanilla whey 33 g
  • FF choc pudding mix 12 g
  • Allulose 1 tbsp
  • Vanilla extract ½ tsp
  • Xanthan gum 1/16 tsp
  • No salt · the mix has plenty

Steps

  1. Blend 75 sec, scrape, 15 more
  2. Into the pint right away, it sets in 3–5 min
  3. Freeze level for 24 hrs
  4. Spin on LITE ICE CREAM
  5. Dry? Add 10 ml milk, RE-SPIN
