Recipe 01 · Side · Instant Pot

Instant Pot
Yellow Rice

Three minutes at pressure, ten minutes of doing nothing, and a pot of rice that makes every other side dish on the table look like it isn't trying.

Serves
4
Active time
~10 min
Pressure
3 min High
Release
10 min NR
§ Ingredients
  • Rice 2 cups
  • Water 2 cups
  • BouillonKnorr cube · halal 1 full cube
  • SeasoningGoya Sazón 1 packet (5g)
  • FatButter To taste
  • SpicesTurmeric and/or cumin Optional
§ Method
01
Sauté and bloom

Turn the Instant Pot on to the Sauté function. Melt the butter, then add the packet of Sazón (and extra spices if using), stirring briefly to bloom the flavors.

02
Toast the rice

Add the 2 cups of rice to the pot. Stir thoroughly so the grains are completely coated in the spiced butter and toast them briefly.

03
Add liquid and bouillon

Pour in the 2 cups of water and add the Knorr cube (crumbling it helps it dissolve faster). Give everything a good stir and scrape any browned bits off the bottom to prevent a burn warning.

04
Pressure cook

Secure the lid. Using the Manual setting, pressure cook on High for exactly 3 minutes.

05
Natural release

Once the 3 minutes are up, leave the Instant Pot alone for a 10-minute natural release. After 10 minutes, manually vent any remaining pressure, open the lid, and fluff the rice with a fork.

Note

The 1:1 rice-to-water ratio is correct for the Instant Pot — it loses far less steam than a stovetop pot. Don't stir after the lid comes off until you've fluffed; that's how you end up with paste.

Recipe 02 · Condiment · No cook

Pepperoncini
Pizza Dipping Sauce

The reason the crusts don't get left on the plate. Briny, garlicky, and better on day two than day one.

Makes
~1½ cups
Active time
~10 min
Rest
30 min
Keeps
~1 week
§ Ingredients
  • Mayonnaise 1 cup
  • PepperonciniDrained and chopped ¼ cup
  • Pepperoncini juice 2 tbsp
  • GarlicMinced 1 clove
  • Fresh parsley 2 tbsp
  • CapersDrained 2 tbsp
§ Method
01
Prep

Drain and finely chop the pepperoncini, mince the garlic, chop the parsley, and drain the capers. Hold back 2 tablespoons of the pepperoncini brine before the rest goes down the sink.

02
Mix

Stir everything into the mayonnaise — pepperoncini, brine, garlic, parsley, and capers — until it's evenly combined and flecked green throughout.

03
Rest

Cover and refrigerate at least 30 minutes so the raw garlic and brine mellow into the mayo. Stir once more before it hits the table.

Note

Chop the pepperoncini and capers fine — you want them suspended in the sauce, not sitting at the bottom of the bowl. If it's too thick to dip a crust into, another splash of brine loosens it without watering down the flavor.

Recipe 03 · Main · Instant Pot

Instant Pot
Daal

Thick, scoopable daal with a browned onion base and a ghee curry-leaf tadka. The onions are the whole recipe — everything after them is assembly.

Serves
4
Dal
Moong + masoor
Pressure
5 min High
Release
10 min NR
§ Ingredients
  • Dal
  • Moong dal½ cup
  • Masoor dal½ cup
  • Base
  • Ghee2 tbsp
  • Cumin seeds1 tsp
  • White onionsFinely chopped1½
  • Green chiliSliced · seeds out for less heat1 small
  • GarlicMinced2 cloves
  • Ginger paste1 tsp
  • Spices
  • Turmeric1 tsp
  • Ground coriander1½ tsp
  • Kashmiri chili powder⅓ tsp
  • Body
  • TomatoesFinely chopped1½
  • SaltTomato stage1 tsp
  • Knorr chicken bouillonCrumbled⅓ cube
  • Water1½ cups
  • Tadka
  • Ghee4 tbsp
  • Cumin seeds1 tsp
  • GarlicThinly sliced4 cloves
  • Fresh curry leaves12
  • Kasuri methiDried fenugreek leaves1 tsp
  • Finish
  • Garam masala1 tsp
  • LemonJuiced½ medium
  • SaltTo taste1 tsp
  • CilantroChopped⅓ cup
§ Method
01
Rinse the dal

Rinse the moong and masoor together until the water runs mostly clear — the masoor throws off extra starch, so expect an extra pass or two. Drain and set aside.

02
Brown the onions

Set the Instant Pot to Sauté (high). Add the 2 tbsp ghee, let it melt, then the cumin seeds — wait for them to crackle. Add the onions and green chili. This is a deep layer of onion, so stir only every 3–4 minutes and let it sit in contact with the metal; stirring more just keeps it wet and steamed. Cook until genuinely golden-brown at the edges. If the pot isn't getting there, finish it in a wide skillet on the stove and transfer back.

03
Bloom the spices

Add the minced garlic and ginger paste, stir 1 minute until fragrant. Add the turmeric, coriander, and Kashmiri chili, stirring constantly for another 30 seconds so the spices hit the fat rather than water. Splash in a tablespoon of water if anything catches.

04
Cook down the tomatoes

Add the tomatoes and 1 tsp salt. Press the tomato against the bottom of the pot with the back of your spoon every few minutes to help it collapse. It's ready when the mixture has thickened and fat pools at the edges — go by that, not the clock.

05
Pressure cook

Turn off Sauté. Stir the crumbled bouillon into the 1½ cups water until dissolved, then add it with the rinsed dal. Scrape the bottom thoroughly — with this little liquid, unscraped fond is the main cause of a burn warning. Seal and cook on High for 5 minutes.

06
Natural release

Let the pressure drop on its own for 10 minutes, then vent the rest. Whisk about 15 seconds — lightly, since the masoor has already broken down and the moong is there for texture.

07
Make the tadka

Heat the 4 tbsp ghee in a small pan until shimmering. Add the cumin seeds and let them crackle, then the sliced garlic until pale gold — pull it the moment it turns. Add the curry leaves with a lid ready, as they spit hard; about 15 seconds until translucent and crisp. Take the pan off the heat, then crumble in the kasuri methi between your palms and let the residual warmth bloom it for 5 seconds.

08
Finish and serve

Stir the garam masala into the daal, then the lemon juice. Taste and add salt as needed. Pour in most of the tadka, stir, and top with the cilantro and the last spoonful.

Note

The onion browning is the step people rush, and it's the one that decides whether this tastes deep or flat. Everything after it takes fifteen minutes; give the onions the time they need and don't drown them by stirring.

Recipe 04 · Main · Oven

Jerk Chicken

Four pounds of boneless thighs, a wet marinade that does all the work overnight, and a short broil at the end to buy back the char that skinless thighs can't give you. Serve with rice and peas.

Adapted from thatgirlcookshealthy · deporecipe
Chicken
4 lb thighs
Marinate
4–8 hrs
Bake
400°F · 20–25
Pull at
175°F
§ Ingredients
  • Boneless chicken thighsSkinless4 lb
  • Marinade
  • Olive oil3 tbsp
  • WalkerswoodMild5 tbsp
  • Green seasoning¼ cup
  • Browning2 tbsp
  • All-purpose seasoning2 tbsp
  • Salt1½ tsp
§ Method
01
Mix the marinade

Combine the wet ingredients in a bowl first — oil, Walkerswood, green seasoning, browning, all-purpose seasoning, and salt — then add the chicken and coat every piece.

02
Marinate

4 to 8 hours. Don't push past 12 with boneless thighs or it goes salty.

03
Bake

400°F for 20–25 minutes, flipping halfway. Use two sheets on separate racks and rotate them at the flip — crowded thighs steam instead of browning.

04
Broil and pull

Broil 2–3 minutes at the end, then pull at 175°F. Skinless thighs have no fat cap, so that final broil is doing the work the skin normally would.

Note

175°F is intentional, not overcooked — thighs are dark meat and the connective tissue needs the extra degrees to break down. Pulling them at 165°F like breast meat gives you chewy chicken.

Recipe 05 · Side · Instant Pot

Rice and Peas

Doubled, all-coconut liquid, extra thyme, butter finish, no scotch bonnet. The beans go on top and stay there — stirring them in before pressure is how you get mush.

Serves
6
Active time
~10 min
Pressure
6 min High
Release
10 min NR
§ Ingredients
  • Aromatics
  • Olive oil1 tbsp
  • Yellow onionFinely chopped1
  • GarlicMinced2 tsp
  • Green onionsFinely chopped2
  • Rice and liquid
  • Long grain white riceRinsed until water runs clear2 cups
  • Full-fat coconut milk360 ml
  • Water120 ml
  • Seasoning
  • Salt1 tsp
  • All-purpose seasoning1 tsp
  • WalkerswoodMild · stands in for the allspice1 tsp
  • Black pepper⅓ tsp
  • On top
  • Red kidney beansDrained and rinsed2 cups
  • Fresh thyme sprigs4
  • Finish
  • Butter2 tbsp
§ Method
01
Sauté the aromatics

Set the pot to Sauté on More. Once it reads Hot, add the olive oil, then the onion, stirring until softened, about 4 minutes. Add the garlic and green onions and stir 30 seconds more.

02
Cancel, cool, deglaze

Press Cancel and let the pot sit a full minute to come off peak heat. Pour in the coconut milk and scrape the base with a wooden spoon until nothing is stuck. With this much coconut fat in the pot, a clean base is the whole difference between dinner and a burn notice.

03
Rice and seasoning

Add the rinsed rice, water, salt, all-purpose seasoning, Walkerswood, and black pepper. Stir until the rice is sitting in liquid with nothing catching on the bottom.

04
Layer beans and thyme

Pour the kidney beans over the rice. Do not stir — they stay layered on top. Lay the thyme sprigs over the beans.

05
Pressure cook

Lid on, valve to Sealing. Pressure Cook on High for 6 minutes — or press Rice and let the cycle run if yours is a newer model.

06
Natural release

Leave the valve alone for 10 minutes, then move it to Venting to release the rest.

07
Butter and fluff

Lift out the thyme stems. Add the butter and fluff with a fork, folding the beans down through the rice. Taste for salt and rest 5 minutes before serving.

Note

All-coconut liquid means there's nothing thin in the pot to protect the base, so the deglaze in step 2 isn't optional. If the pot still throws a burn warning, it's fond you missed, not the ratio.

Recipe 06 · Pasta · Stand mixer

Fresh Fettuccine

Makes ~440g of dough — two generous or three modest servings. A dry-looking dough that comes good on the bench, rolled to a thin 1.0mm sheet.

Makes
~440g
Knead
8–10 min
Rest
30 min +
Final sheet
Level 4 · ~1mm
§ Ingredients
  • All-purpose flourPlus extra for dusting2 cups · 300g
  • Eggs3
  • Salt1 tsp
  • Olive oil1 tbsp + 1 tsp
  • Water1 tbsp
§ Method
01
Mix

Whisk the flour and salt, mound it, and hollow a well in the center. Eggs, oil, and water into the well.

02
Bring it together

Beat the eggs with a fork, pulling in flour from the inner wall a bit at a time. When it's too stiff to fork, switch to hands and press into a shaggy ball.

03
Knead 8–10 minutes

Heel of your hand, fold, quarter turn, repeat. Done when it's smooth and a poke springs back slowly.

04
Rest

30 minutes minimum, wrapped tight at room temp. An hour is better.

05
Roll

Divide into 4 and cover what you're not using. Mixer at speed 5–6. Flatten a piece to ~1cm and run it at level 8 six to eight times, folding in half and rotating 90° between passes. Then single passes: 7 → 6 → 5 → 4. Halve the sheet crosswise once it's longer than your forearm.

06
Rest the sheets

15 minutes on a floured towel. The surface should feel like soft leather — dry to the touch but still flexible.

07
Cut

Through the fettuccine cutter. Flour the ribbons immediately and coil them into loose nests.

Notes

With only 1 tbsp of water, the dough will look too dry when you first bring it together. Don't add water back yet — knead for 2–3 minutes first and let the flour hydrate. Only add liquid a teaspoon at a time if it still won't come together after that.

Level 4 gives you a ~1.0mm sheet, which is more fragile than 1.3mm was. Support it with your free hand coming off the roller and cut it in half sooner than you think.

Cook time drops slightly at this thickness — start checking at 90 seconds.

Recipe 07 · Main · Skillet

Creamy Cajun-Style
Sausage Fettuccine

What the fresh fettuccine is for. A half-cream, half-milk sauce that needs a gentler hand than an all-cream one, built on the fond from browning everything else first.

Serves
2
Sauce
½ cream ½ milk
Pasta
2–3 min
Total
~25 min
§ Ingredients
  • Fresh fettuccineRecipe 061 batch
  • Skillet
  • Jalapeño cheddar chicken sausageSliced into coins2
  • Cremini mushroomsSliced thick5
  • Green bell pepperStrips½
  • Red bell pepperStrips½
  • Yellow onionSliced½
  • GarlicMinced3 cloves
  • Olive oil1 tbsp
  • Seasoning
  • Salt1½ tsp
  • Onion powder1 tsp
  • Garlic powder1 tsp
  • Adobo1 tsp
  • Red chili flakes½ tsp
  • Black pepperTo taste
  • Sauce
  • Heavy cream½ cup
  • Milk½ cup
  • Butter1 tbsp
  • Grated parmesan½ cup
§ Method
01
Pasta water on

Large pot, salted, rolling boil. Leave it going.

02
Mushrooms · 4–5 min

Olive oil in a wide skillet, medium-high. Single layer, don't stir, don't salt. Flip when the undersides are properly browned.

03
Sausage · 2–3 min

Push the mushrooms aside and add the coins. They're already cooked, so you're just after browned edges. Leave the fond in the pan.

04
Onion and peppers · 3–4 min

Onion translucent, peppers softened but still snappy.

05
Garlic and seasoning · 45 sec

Heat to medium. Garlic, onion powder, garlic powder, adobo, chili flakes, black pepper. Stir constantly — garlic burns fast.

06
Cream sauce · 6–7 min

Cream and milk in, scrape up the fond. Barely-trembling simmer, no real bubbles. Reduce until it coats the back of a spoon. This takes longer than a straight-cream sauce because half of it is milk.

07
Pasta · 2–3 min

Into the boiling water, stir immediately. Done when it floats and turns slightly translucent. Reserve a mug of pasta water before draining.

08
Combine

Heat completely off. Butter and parmesan in, stir until smooth. Pasta in, toss hard 30–60 seconds with splashes of pasta water until the sauce clings to the strands.

Notes

The half-and-half dairy split means less fat holding the emulsion — keep the heat low, and the butter and parmesan at the end are doing real structural work, not just flavor.

Adobo is salt-based, so it's stacking on the 1½ tsp. Taste before each splash of pasta water rather than after, and use plain hot water to loosen if it's getting salty.

Fix sauce thickness before the pasta goes in. Fresh pasta keeps drinking liquid and won't wait.
