Talk track

Open

This is a follow-up to the read I gave on Aug 17. We now have more than a month of actual numbers.

Where we are

Today we have 305 RSVPs against a goal of 950. That is 32% of the goal, and we have 35 days to go.

Mix is up

First, the good news: the mix is better. BCM has 94 of the 305 RSVPs, so 31% of the room. On Aug 17, BCM had 18 of 82, which was 22%. Since then, BCM grew 5.2 times and the total grew 3.7 times. The separate BCM track does what we said it would do.

By source

By source: apply to attend has 96, BCM 94, partnerships 52, community 24, node 20, TR 10, and speakers 9.

Volume is short

Volume is the problem. Since Aug 17, we get about 6 new RSVPs a day. To get to 950, we need about 18 a day from now. That is three times our current pace.

Proposal

At the current pace, we land near 510. With a normal surge in the last two weeks, we land near 660. So I propose that we reset the target to 700. At our current mix, that gives us about 216 BCM registrations. At a 55% show rate, 700 RSVPs puts about 385 people in the room.

Next

Two next steps. Tomorrow I have a sales kit session with the team. Next week I will send a meeting invite to plan invite activities that are not email.
