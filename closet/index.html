Closet.

A redesign of a friend's sliding-door closet. Four white melamine shelves on rails on the left, double hang on the right, and a top shelf in the hidden space above the doors. Everything hangs on the studs, with no centre panel. 73-1/2 x 24 x 96 in inside.

02

Why it's built this way

The closet had one shelf and one rod at 64 in, and a lot of empty wall. It holds mostly clothes, and the sliding panels stay as they are.

  • One half at a time. The two sliding panels are 38 in wide and overlap in the middle, so only half the closet opens at once. Each half gets one job: shelves on the left, hanging on the right.
  • Built around the studs. The studs are at 17 and 33 in from the left wall, so the shelf side is 33 in wide and the hanging side is 40-1/2 in.
  • Shelves on rails. Two vertical rails on the studs at 17 and 33 in hold the four side shelves. The heights can change later without new holes.
  • Double hang without wall brackets. The upper rod sits on two shelf and rod brackets. The lower rod hangs from it, so the rail can use the stud at 33 in.
  • The space above the doors. The doors stop at 81 in but the ceiling is at 96. A top shelf at 84 in uses that space for light bins.
  • Stiffened melamine. 5/8 in melamine bends more than plywood. A 1x2 under the front edge and a cleat at the wall end keep each side shelf flat.
  • Everything goes into studs. Loaded shelves and rods pull on the wall. Drywall anchors are only for the wall cleats, where the load is straight down.
  • Fits in a CR-V. The store cuts the 97 in boards. The longest piece is 40-1/4 in.

08

Brackets and tools

2 rails, 12 brackets, a rod and a double hang rod. Each drawing is a side view, to scale. In the 3D model, tap a part for its size and job.

  • Rails and rail brackets2 + 8x

    Everbilt vertical rails, 68 in, and 8 in brackets of the same brand. Check the package: 50 lb or more per shelf

    1 rail on each stud at 17 and 33 in, from about 5 to 73 in from the floor. #10 x 2-1/2 in screws at every hole.

    2 brackets under each side shelf. Both rail bottoms at the same height, each rail plumb.

  • Shelf and rod brackets2x

    Everbilt Regular Duty Shelf and Rod Bracket, white. 12-3/4 in deep, for 10 to 14 in shelves, 500 lb per pair into studs

    On the studs at 33 and 65 in. They hold the top shelf and the upper rod.

    Top arm at 84 in. 3 #10 x 2-1/2 in screws each, into the stud. Plumb before the last screw.

  • L brackets, top shelf2x

    Steel shelf brackets, 8 in arm, rated 50 lb or more

    On the studs at 17 and 49 in, under the top shelf.

    Short, so they clear the hanger hooks on the upper rod.

  • Upper rod and double hang rod1 + 1x

    Steel closet rod, 1-5/16 in, 48 in, and an adjustable double hang closet rod

    Cut the upper rod to about 41-1/4 in and lock it in each hook with a #8 x 1/2 in sheet metal set screw.

    The double hang rod hooks over it, with the lower rod at about 40 in. No wall brackets for the lower rod.

  • Flat mending plates2x

    Flat steel mending plates, about 1 x 6 in, with their short screws

    On top of the top shelf, across the joint of the 2 pieces.

    The joint sits on the shelf and rod bracket at 33 in.

09

Build steps

The store cuts the boards. The rest is one afternoon at the closet.

Before: the parts

01
Cut the shelves and 1x2s

From each of 2 boards, cut 2 side shelves at 33 in. From the third, cut 40-1/4 and 32-3/4 in for the top shelf. Cut the 1x2s into 4 strips at 31 in and 5 cleats at 10 in.

02
Stiffen the side shelves

Glue a 1x2 strip on edge under the front of each side shelf, 1 in from the left end. Screw down through the shelf into it, countersunk, and cover the heads with white caps.

At the closet: the install

03
Clear it out and mark

Lift the 2 sliding panels off the track. Take out the old shelf and rod and fill the holes. Mark the studs at 17 and 33 in and check for 49 and 65 in. Mark 84 in for the top shelf and 5 in for the bottom of the rails.

04
Rails

1 rail on each stud at 17 and 33 in, bottoms at 5 in. Put in the top screw, make the rail plumb, then screw into the stud at every hole.

05
Top shelf brackets

Shelf and rod brackets on the studs at 33 and 65 in, L brackets on the studs at 17 and 49 in. All top arms at 84 in, level across all four.

06
Wall cleats

On the left wall, the top of each cleat at the bottom of its shelf: 84, 64, 48, 32 and 16 in. Into the corner stud, or metal anchors.

07
Top shelf

Set the 2 pieces on the brackets, with the joint on the bracket at 33 in. Screw up through the bracket holes and join the pieces on top with the 2 mending plates.

08
Side shelves

Clip 2 rail brackets in at 64, 48, 32 and 16 in. Set each shelf on its brackets and its wall cleat, then screw up through the bracket holes.

09
Rods

Cut the rod to about 41-1/4 in, set it in the 2 hooks and lock it with a set screw in each. Hook the double hang rod over it and level the lower rod at about 40 in.

10
Panels back on

Set the sliding panels back on the track and check they slide freely. Load heavy items low and to the back of the side shelves. The top shelf is for light bins only.

Before buying

Read the load rating on the rail package. You need 50 lb or more per shelf. If it is less, use the heavy duty rails.

Check the stud at 65 in before you buy the second shelf and rod bracket.
