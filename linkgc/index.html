        <h3>Fireside Chat with Dan Ives</h3>
        <p><strong>Dan Ives</strong>Partner &amp; Senior Managing Director, Analyst, Yorkville Ives &amp; Co</p>
        <p><strong>Andrew McCormick (Moderator)</strong>Head of Institutional &amp; Market Development, Chainlink Labs</p>

    <div class="sessions">
      <details class="session">
        <summary><span class="t">From Market Hours to Market Infrastructure: Enabling 24/7 Collateral Mobility</span><span class="pm" aria-hidden="true">+</span></summary>
        <p>Collateral markets are entering a new era. In this session, leaders from traditional finance and blockchain infrastructure will unpack how the industry is laying the foundation for near real-time, 24/7 collateral management across global markets and blockchains. The discussion will explore how shared onchain infrastructure can modernize margining, settlement, valuation, and collateral optimization while improving capital efficiency and reducing operational friction. Attendees will gain insight into the convergence of traditional market infrastructure and decentralized systems, the role of interoperable data and orchestration layers, and what production-grade tokenized finance looks like as institutions move from pilots to live deployment.</p>
      </details>
      <details class="session">
        <summary><span class="t">From Hype to Infrastructure: Making Tokenized Assets Work</span><span class="pm" aria-hidden="true">+</span></summary>
        <p>Tokenized assets have captured attention, but what does it actually take to make them work at scale? This panel dives into the lifecycle of digital assets, examining the operational, technological, and regulatory considerations of servicing digital assets after issuance.</p>
      </details>
      <details class="session">
        <summary><span class="t">Tokenization at Scale: Building the Full Financial Lifecycle Onchain</span><span class="pm" aria-hidden="true">+</span></summary>
        <p>Tokenization's shift from experimental programs to production-ready systems introduces new challenges. The real complexity of tokenized finance lies in managing the full lifecycle: from origination and issuance to distribution, servicing, and redemption. In this panel, we'll explore how responsibilities are divided across the stack, from issuers and banks to infrastructure providers and oracle networks, and how coordination across these layers impacts security, reliability, compliance, and performance.</p>
      </details>
      <details class="session">
        <summary><span class="t">Data, Oracles, and the Future of Financial Truth</span><span class="pm" aria-hidden="true">+</span></summary>
        <p>As financial markets move onchain, data quality becomes increasingly critical to the success of global markets. From pricing and NAV to interest rates and identity, secure data is the backbone of onchain finance. At the center of this shift are oracles: the infrastructure delivering offchain financial data into blockchain environments. This session will explore how institutional-grade data is being brought onchain securely and what it means for the future of financial infrastructure.</p>
      </details>
      <details class="session">
        <summary><span class="t">The Future of Money and Markets in an Onchain Dollar Era</span><span class="pm" aria-hidden="true">+</span></summary>
        <p>This panel explores how stablecoins, tokenized cash, and programmable infrastructure are reshaping monetary transmission, reserve demand, collateral mobility, bank funding, deposit behavior, and sovereign cash operations. Geared toward a capital markets and institutional finance audience, the discussion focuses on how public sector operating models may evolve as tokenized money and tokenized government liabilities become more systemically relevant.</p>
      </details>
      <details class="session">
        <summary><span class="t">Parallel Systems, One Market: The Future of Interoperable Finance</span><span class="pm" aria-hidden="true">+</span></summary>
        <p>As digital assets and tokenized finance scale, markets are fragmenting across blockchains, platforms, and existing systems. The next phase of financial innovation will be defined by the infrastructure that connects them. This panel explores how cross-chain interoperability enables seamless asset movement, reducing fragmentation, and driving the convergence of TradFi and DeFi into a unified global market by connecting to existing infrastructure.</p>
      </details>
      <details class="session">
        <summary><span class="t">The Road Ahead: How Leaders Are Shaping the Next Phase of Digital Finance</span><span class="pm" aria-hidden="true">+</span></summary>
        <p>After years of experimentation, digital assets are entering a new phase where real-world execution and measurable outcomes take center stage. In this executive panel, CEOs will share how they are navigating the next 24 months of digital asset development: where they are doubling down, where they are pulling back, and how they are separating signal from noise.</p>
      </details>
      <details class="session">
        <summary><span class="t">Securing the Agentic Economy: Why AI Needs Cryptographic Truth</span><span class="pm" aria-hidden="true">+</span></summary>
        <p>AI and blockchains are moving into live market infrastructure. As autonomous AI agents transition from generating content to executing economic actions, they face a critical bottleneck: today's internet and financial systems were not designed for autonomous software. This panel explores the emerging infrastructure of the agentic economy. Industry leaders will discuss how agents can safely transact, coordinate, and create value across digital markets, and what infrastructure must exist before autonomous commerce becomes mainstream.</p>
      </details>
