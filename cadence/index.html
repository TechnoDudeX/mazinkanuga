01 · Read Me

What changed,
and what is blocked

Version 2. Updated 26 August 2026 by Mazin Kanuga after the Sibos + Link NYC cadence sync. Written in Simplified Technical English.

What changed in version 2

  • Source: the cadence sync of 26 August 2026, 08:28 ET. Present: Mazin Kanuga, Hod Klein, Sam Ewen, Ashley O'Brien, Resham F.
  • 1One email to all 500 contacts is dead. The first Sibos send went to 500 matched accounts and produced one meeting. Hod Klein gave Mazin unrestricted control of the segmentation: 'you have free reign to cut this whichever way you want'.
  • 2Every Sibos wave is now three sends, not one: Europe, the USA and APAC. Each send goes from a named person, in the local time zone, with a case study for that region.
  • 3The sender is a person, not the brand. Use David for Europe, Frank Seibold for the USA, Nikki for APAC and Fernando for tier 2 and tier 3. Keep the email plain. Ask for a reply, not a form.
  • 4Tier 2 and tier 3 are now in the Sibos programme. Hod Klein: 'we actually go out to the tier two and three, that's for sure'.
  • 5A VIP send from Sergey Nazarov is approved. It asks the named account to reply. Amal Hijazi's team answers the replies.
  • 6The Sibos meeting target is 75. Hod Klein stated it on 26 August. Most of it comes from 1:1 sales outreach. Nobody has stated the share that email must produce. See the tab 'Funnel Model'.
  • 7Link NYC gets three panel emails. Each one carries one panel: 'The Road Ahead', 'From market hours to market infrastructure' and 'Liquidity standards for on-chain markets'.
  • 8Link NYC gets a networking email. An APAC client told Resham that networking is what he wants from the day.
  • 9The post-event set is now five emails for each event: thank you, sorry we missed you, five best moments with video clips, the care package, and the feedback survey.
  • 10Do not put the Link NYC registration link in a general Sibos email. Ashley O'Brien: 'no, no, because it's invite only'. A VIP-only exception is possible. It is not agreed.

Open items that block a send

  • BLOCKEDthe seat cap for Link NYC. Two people wrote 'only 50 seats remain' copy. Nobody owns that number. Do not write scarcity copy until Amal Hijazi confirms the real cap in writing.
  • BLOCKEDthe destination for the video clips. Hod Klein is 'somewhat inclined' to the website with one blog for each clip. That is not a decision. The five-best-moments email cannot be built until it is one.
  • BLOCKEDthe Salesforce match. 44 JP Morgan contacts are on the Sibos list and not in Salesforce. Behind them are 'thousands of new people who are not in our CRM'. Hod Klein and Mazin do this dig offline. It has no date.
  • BLOCKEDthe segmentation rule for Link NYC. Amal Hijazi decided on 14 August that all groups get the same email. Hod Klein gave free reign on 26 August. The two rules disagree. Ask Amal and Hod which one applies.
  • OPENthe share of the 75 Sibos meetings that email must produce. Propose the split before somebody assigns the whole 75.
  • OPENfour Link NYC numbers do not reconcile: 35 BCM RSVPs, a 450 BCM RSVP target, about 140 registrations, and 300 to 400 people in the room. See the tab 'Funnel Model'.

What this file is

  • Tab 'Sibos Cadence' — the meeting-request emails to the matched delegates. Event: 28 September to 1 October 2026, Miami.
  • Tab 'Link NYC Marketing' — the invitation waves to persons who are not registered. Event: 29 October 2026, New York.
  • Tab 'Link NYC Registrants' — the reminder, day-of and post-event emails to persons who are registered.
  • Tab 'Subject Lines' — two subject lines for each marketing email. Test one against the other.
  • Tab 'Funnel Model' — the number of invitations that each attendance target needs. Change the blue cells.
  • Tab 'Naming and UTM' — the campaign names and the UTM values. Use them for all sends.

How to use this file

  • 1Copy this file into Google Sheets. Share it with Hod, Sherin, Margaux, Amal and Sam Ewen. Sam Ewen owns events, so he now sees both programmes.
  • 2Fill in the result columns one day after each send. Two days is better. Time zones delay the data.
  • 3Do not change a send date without a note in the Status column.
  • 4Blue text is an input. You can change it. Black text is a formula. Do not change it.
  • 5A green row is an email that went out. A blue row is new in version 2. An amber row is blocked.
  • 6Post the send analytics to Slack on the same day, as a screenshot, framed as a test. Hod Klein asked for this habit on 25 August.

Rules that apply to every send

  • Do not send on the 11th of September. Do not post on the 11th of September. Hod Klein, 25 August.
  • Do not send on the 13th of a month. Sergey does not accept the number 13.
  • Prefer the 7th of a month, and 07:00, when the date is a free choice.
  • Send in the recipient's local time zone. Do not send one time to all regions.
  • Apply a city or state filter for Link NYC. It is an event in one room in New York. The send of 25 August had no geography filter and it opened at 8 per cent against 28 per cent.
  • Suppress all persons who are registered. Suppress all unsubscribes and all hard bounces.
  • For Link NYC, upload the new contacts to the Bizzabo back end before each send. If you do not, the recipients get the apply-and-vet page instead of the automatic confirmation.
  • Copy the URLs from the browser address bar. Do not copy them from a document. Chainlink wraps outbound URLs and the send fails without a message.
  • Set the utm_campaign value before you schedule. See the tab 'Naming and UTM'.
  • Do not bulk-export the CRM to build a list. It starts a security alert the same night.
  • Do not name Bottom Line. It is not announced. Hod Klein, 26 August.

Assumptions in this file

  • Sibos runs from Monday 28 September to Thursday 1 October 2026, in Miami. Source: Sibos weekly, 19 August 2026.
  • Link NYC runs on Thursday 29 October 2026, in New York. Source: Amal Hijazi 1:1, 14 August 2026.
  • Mazin is away from 1 September to 10 September. He builds and schedules every send in that period before he goes.
  • The Sibos meeting target is 75. Source: Hod Klein, cadence sync, 26 August 2026.
  • All conversion rates in the funnel model are estimates. Replace them with the wave results.
  • The Sibos email of 18 August has one reply. The open rate, the click rate and the number of sends are still not known.
  • The Link NYC email of 25 August opened at about 8 per cent against about 28 per cent for the comparable send. Source: Hod Klein, 25 August 2026.
